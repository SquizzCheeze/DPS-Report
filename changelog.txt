DPS Report Changelog
====================

V1.28

Fixed:

- A Holy Priest's death taken by Spirit of Redemption was missing from the
  Deaths list in Mythic+, though the key counted it and took the time.
  They become an angel rather than dying - and with Restitution never die
  at all - so nothing flagged them dead. When the key's death count goes
  up with nobody seen dying, the death is now credited to the group's
  priest healer.

New:

- Deaths in time order (Meter settings, on by default): the Deaths list
  shows each death on its own row in the order they happened, with when it
  happened (minutes into the key or fight) where the count used to be.
  Hovering a row shows that death's hits, and clicking opens that death in
  Squizzcap. Untick it for one row per player with a count, as before. The
  end-of-run summary and saved runs still count per player.
