DPS Report Changelog
====================

V1.8

New:

- Finishing a Mythic+ key now announces a summary of the run instead of a
  single metric. It reads:

    --- Ara-Kara, City of Echoes +12 completed in 28:41 ---
    MVP: Bobhealz
    Top DMG: Squizzcheeze (48.2M)
    Top Healing: Bobhealz (31.7M)
    Top Interrupts: Tankboi (14)
    Top Dispels: Bobhealz (9)
    Least Avoidable DMG: Squizzcheeze (412K)
    Deaths: 3 total (most: Tankboi with 2)

  Every line can be switched off on its own under Auto Report, so you can cut
  it down to just the parts your group cares about. Lines with nothing to say
  are dropped automatically -- no "Top Dispels" line on a run with no dispels,
  and no avoidable-damage line if nobody took any.

- MVP is a role-weighted score. Each player is measured on their share of the
  group's damage, healing, interrupts and dispels, weighted by what their role
  is actually responsible for, then penalised for dying and for taking
  avoidable damage. A healer topping healing beats a DPS topping damage on a
  run where the healer also dispelled and never died.

- Deaths now name who died most, not just the group total.

- All of this comes from data the addon was already collecting at the end of a
  run. No combat log parsing was added and nothing extra is tracked during the
  fight, so there is no performance cost.

Changed:

- The settings panel is split into categories down the left side --
  Formatting, Auto Report, Mythic+, Meter, Profiles, Tools -- rather than one
  long scroll. The panel is wider to fit the sidebar; the options themselves
  are unchanged and in the same order within each category.

- The "Auto Report Type" dropdown is gone, replaced by the per-line toggles
  described above. If you had it set to something other than DPS, you will now
  get the summary instead; switch off the lines you do not want.

Fixed:

- Reporting a saved Mythic+ segment while the meter was set to Avoidable
  Damage reported Damage Taken instead. Those are different metrics and the
  segment held both -- it was simply reading the wrong one.

- Reporting a saved segment set to Enemy Damage Taken reported the group's own
  damage done under an "Enemy Damage Taken" heading. Enemy damage is not saved
  into segments at all, so this now says there is no data for that mode rather
  than showing the wrong number.

- The slash command help was wrong. /dps has only ever opened the settings
  panel; the arguments the header comment listed (/dps hps, /dps overall,
  /dps whisper Name and the rest) were never implemented, and /dpsreport was
  never registered. The documentation now matches what the addon does.
