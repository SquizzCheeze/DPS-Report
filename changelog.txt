DPS Report Changelog
====================

V1.26

New:

- Raid boss pulls are saved. Every kill and wipe on a raid boss is stored
  like a Mythic+ run, named "Boss (Heroic) - Wipe #3", so you can pick any
  pull from a meter's session list afterwards and compare attempts: who did
  what on pull 7 against pull 12. The last 20 pulls are kept, separately
  from your ten saved keys so a raid night never pushes those out, and
  "Last Run" features still mean your last key. Turn it off under Mythic+
  in the settings.
