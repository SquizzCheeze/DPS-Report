DPS Report Changelog
====================

V1.24

Fixed:

- At the end of a Mythic+ run the Overall meter showed nonsense: a timer like
  "26871:01" for a 26-minute key, and DPS around a thousand times too low --
  "227" next to 367 million damage, where it should have read 227K. Damage
  totals were always right, and Overall read correctly during the run.

  Once the key completes, the game reports the Overall session's length in
  milliseconds instead of seconds, and works out each player's per-second
  numbers from that same wrong length. DPS Report now spots when the reported
  length is impossible -- longer than the time since you reset, or far longer
  than all the fights it contains -- and scales it back. That corrects the
  timer, and DPS and HPS for the meter, its reports, the breakdown window and
  the run saved at the end of the key.

  The same wrong length also made the death count on Overall fall back to the
  game's own list, which counts Feign Death as a death. It uses DPS Report's
  own count again.
