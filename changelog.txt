DPS Report Changelog
====================

V1.27

New:

- Click a player on the Deaths list to open their death in Squizzcap, our
  death recap addon: what killed them, their health over the last hits,
  and everything that hit them that fight. Needs Squizzcap 1.3.0 or later;
  without it, a click opens the usual breakdown as before. The bar's
  tooltip says when a click will open Squizzcap.
- The Deaths list's tooltip and breakdown now show the hits that killed
  each player, killing blow first, with how much each did and the health
  they were at when it landed. Read from Blizzard's death recap, so it
  works without Squizzcap too. Not kept for saved runs. A player who died
  more than once: the tooltip shows their last death and says how many
  there were, and the breakdown lists every one, newest first.
