DPS Report Changelog
====================

V1.29

New:

- DPS Graph: click the G on any meter's title bar (or type /dpsgraph). Every
  fight this session is recorded, one bar per second, for you and each group
  member - DPS, total damage, HPS or total healing. Hover the graph for the
  value at that moment; < and > step through your last 10 fights, click the
  player name to switch player, and - / + change the range.
  The game hides these numbers from addons during combat, so the bars are
  drawn by the game itself and the range is a guess until the fight ends,
  when it rescales from the final totals. Graphs last until you /reload or
  log out; the game does not let addons save them. Two players with the same
  class and spec can't be told apart mid-fight, so they are left out of that
  fight's graph.
- (Test) Import a finished key OR raid boss pull (kill or wipe) from your
  combat log into the graph, with the real per-second numbers the game hides
  during play: run the exporter script, then Import > Ctrl+V. It works while
  the game is running. Boss pulls are shaded, and Avg 1s/3s/5s smooths the
  spikes.
- Threat meter: click the T on any meter's title bar (or /dpsthreat). Your
  group and their pets, ranked by threat on your target - whoever has aggro
  is marked and listed first, and your own bar turns orange when you're
  close to pulling and red when you have it. Right-click its title for
  target or focus, hiding out of combat (on by default) and locking it.
- New option (Mythic+ page): Log combat to file in dungeons and raids. Turns
  WoW's combat logging on when you enter one and off when you leave - but
  only if it turned it on: logging started by Archon, /combatlog or another
  addon is left alone, and if you switch it off yourself it stays off for
  that visit. Off by default.
- Imported keys and pulls are SAVED (the last 20, plus any you pin to keep
  forever) and listed under Keys, so they survive /reload and logging out.
- Compare: pick another saved key or pull to show its bars in grey beside the
  current one - same player, shared timeline - with both totals, the
  difference and the averages.
