DPS Report Changelog
====================

V1.29

New:

- DPS Graph: click the G on any meter's title bar (or type /dpsgraph). Every
  fight this session is recorded, one bar per second, for you and each group
  member - DPS, total damage, HPS or total healing. Hover the graph for the
  value at that moment; < and > step through your last 10 fights, click the
  player name to switch player, and - / + change the range.
  The game hides these numbers from addons during combat, so the bars are
  drawn by the game itself and the range is a guess until the fight ends,
  when it rescales from the final totals. Graphs last until you /reload or
  log out; the game does not let addons save them. Two players with the same
  class and spec can't be told apart mid-fight, so they are left out of that
  fight's graph.
- (Test) Import a finished key OR raid boss pull (kill or wipe) from your
  combat log into the graph, with the real per-second numbers the game hides
  during play: copy it with DPS Report Exporter (below), then Import > Ctrl+V.
  It works while the game is running. Boss pulls are shaded, and Avg 1s/3s/5s
  smooths the spikes.
- Fight history: click the H on any meter's title bar (or /dpshistory).
  Every boss pull ("Boss (Difficulty) Kill/Wipe") and every whole Mythic+ run
  ("Dungeon +8 (timed)", trash included) is kept - the last 200, per
  character - with each player's DPS, damage, HPS, healing and interrupts.
  Search narrows the list as you type (a boss, a dungeon, +8, heroic, wipe, a
  date). Click one to see it; right-click another to compare - every number
  gets a green/red % against it. The numbers are the game meter's own: a
  run matches its Overall for the key (DPS over time in combat, with the key
  timer shown beside it), a boss pull matches that pull's segment.
- Personal bests: your best DPS and best total on every boss you kill, by
  difficulty (Personal bests tab), with a line in chat when you beat one.
- Threat meter: click the T on any meter's title bar (or /dpsthreat). Your
  group and their pets, ranked by threat on your target - whoever has aggro
  is marked and listed first, and your own bar turns orange when you're
  close to pulling and red when you have it. Right-click its title for
  target or focus, hiding out of combat (on by default) and locking it.
- New option (Mythic+ page): Log combat to file in dungeons and raids. Turns
  WoW's combat logging on when you enter one and off when you leave - but
  only if it turned it on: logging started by Archon, /combatlog or another
  addon is left alone, and if you switch it off yourself it stays off for
  that visit. Off by default.
- By ability: on an imported key or pull, each second of a player's graph
  is split into their top abilities (and Other), with a coloured legend;
  hover for the biggest three at that moment. Works for damage and healing.
- The graph window got a new layout (imported keys and pulls): a filled,
  stacked area chart instead of bars, a sidebar listing everyone in the run
  with spec icons and a Find a character box, All characters (everyone
  stacked in class colours), and an Event log of the player's casts and
  aura changes with its own filter. Timeline is the old Casts toggle.
- DPS Report Exporter, a free companion app for Windows: it sits in the
  tray while you play and copies every boss pull and finished key the moment
  it ends, with a notification - in game just Import, Ctrl+V. Download it
  from https://squizzcheeze.github.io/DPS-Report/
- Find a character also finds you by name; your row shows your name.
- Timeline: on an imported key or pull, lanes under the ability graph -
  one lane per spell with its icon and name, an icon at every cast, and a bar
  wherever its DoT, HoT, buff or channel was up. Window (all/15s/30s/1m/2m)
  zooms it; the mouse wheel scrolls it (over the names: scrolls the spells)
  and clicking the graph jumps there. Hover a lane for its casts and uptime.
- Imported keys and pulls are SAVED (the last 20, plus any you pin to keep
  forever) and listed under Keys, so they survive /reload and logging out.
- Compare: pick another saved key or pull to show its bars in grey beside the
  current one - same player, shared timeline - with both totals, the
  difference and the averages.
